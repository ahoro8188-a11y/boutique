Small batch, made with intention

Wren & Co. started as a market stall selling hand-picked vintage finds and pieces from local makers. Today, we still work directly with small studios and independent designers — no mass production, no filler.

Every drop is limited. Once it's gone, it's gone — and that's exactly how we like it.
